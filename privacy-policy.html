Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

The short version: StockTracker doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to us. Your watchlist stays on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers, because StockTracker doesn't have any.

What This App Does

StockTracker is a stock watchlist app. You add ticker symbols, and the app displays real-time and after-hours prices by connecting directly from your device to Finnhub, a third-party market data provider, using an API key you provide.

What We Collect

Nothing. StockTracker does not operate any servers, does not have user accounts, and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, usage data, or analytics about you or your device.

What Stays On Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your watchlist symbols and share quantities
  • Your Finnhub API key(s)
  • Your app preferences (theme, view density, update frequency, and similar settings)

This data is stored using standard on-device storage (SQLite and secure storage) and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

StockTracker connects directly from your device to the following third-party services. We do not act as an intermediary — your device communicates with these services directly, using your own API key where applicable:

  • Finnhub — provides real-time and after-hours stock price data and company news, using the API key you supply. See Finnhub's own privacy policy and terms for how they handle any data associated with your API key.
  • Apple, Inc. — handles all in-app purchase transactions through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information; that's governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any in the first place.

Children's Privacy

StockTracker is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds analytics or crash reporting — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at support@homeinventoryhq.com.

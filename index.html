Common Questions

Why do I need my own Finnhub API key?

StockTracker doesn't run its own servers — your watchlist and price data connect directly from your device to Finnhub's free market data API. A free key takes about 30 seconds to get at finnhub.io, no credit card required.

How do I import my portfolio?

From the main screen, tap Import and choose a CSV file with Symbol and Quantity columns. StockTracker automatically skips option contracts and rows it can't recognize, and shows a summary of what was imported.

What does the one-time unlock include?

The free version supports up to 3 symbols. A single one-time purchase removes that limit and unlocks CSV import/export — no subscriptions, ever.

I bought the unlock but it's not showing on a new device.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
